## Project Overview

This repository contains the Master's thesis developed for the **M.Sc. in Probability and Statistics** at CIMAT. The research studies a modified $\Lambda$-Wright-Fisher population model that incorporates multi-type frequency-dependent selection mechanisms alongside both extreme and non-extreme reproductive events, formulated through the notion of coloring rules.

By deriving the scaling limits of the discrete process, the work establishes convergence to the unique solution of a limiting stochastic differential equation (SDE) and characterizes the qualitative behavior of the system in the vanishing-noise limit.

## Objectives & Methodology

- **Stochastic Modeling & Scaling Limits:** Extending the $\Lambda$-Wright-Fisher process with general frequency-dependent selection via coloring rules and proving convergence in the scaling limit to a well-posed stochastic differential equation (SDE).
- **Dynamical & Stability Analysis:** Analyzing equilibrium points, stability properties, and non-transitive dynamics (such as Rock–Paper–Scissors cycles) of the limiting deterministic system under a vanishing-noise assumption.
- **Stochastic Simulation:** Developing numerical solvers and stochastic simulations in Python to study sample trajectories and cyclic behavior of the limiting SDE.
